
WEATHER SAFETY AND ADVISORY KNOWLEDGE

1. HEAVY RAINFALL
Heavy rainfall can cause waterlogging, flooding, and poor visibility.
Avoid travelling through flooded roads.
Keep emergency supplies and drinking water ready.
Follow official weather warnings.

2. THUNDERSTORMS
During thunderstorms, stay indoors and avoid open fields.
Do not shelter under isolated trees.
Stay away from electrical equipment and metal objects.
Avoid using wired electrical appliances during lightning.

3. HEATWAVES
During extreme heat, drink sufficient water.
Avoid direct sunlight during peak afternoon hours.
Wear lightweight and light-coloured clothing.
Watch for symptoms of heat exhaustion.

4. COLD WEATHER
Wear warm clothing during cold conditions.
Protect children and elderly people from prolonged exposure.
Use heating equipment safely and maintain ventilation.

5. STRONG WINDS
Secure loose outdoor objects.
Avoid standing near damaged trees and electrical poles.
Follow official warnings before travelling.

6. GENERAL WEATHER PRECAUTIONS
Check weather forecasts before travelling.
Follow advisories issued by official authorities.
Keep emergency contact information accessible.
Never ignore severe weather warnings.
